# Design Specification: Retro Warmth Theme

## Overview
A vintage-inspired color palette emphasizing retro shades of red, yellow, and green. This design evokes a nostalgic 70s/80s feel, perfect for a bold, energetic, and warm user interface.

## Color Palette

### Primary Colors
*   **Retro Red:** `#D9381E`
    *   *Usage:* Primary buttons, critical alerts, and striking highlights.
*   **Retro Yellow:** `#E8B923`
    *   *Usage:* Warnings, secondary highlights, badges, and attention-grabbing elements.
*   **Retro Green:** `#4B8B3B`
    *   *Usage:* Success states, positive actions, and accent backgrounds.

### Neutral Colors
*   **Background (Light Mode):** `#FDF5E6` (Cream/Off-White)
*   **Background (Dark Mode):** `#1A1A1A` (Vintage Charcoal)
*   **Text (Primary):** `#2C2C2C` (Deep Gray)
*   **Text (Secondary):** `#666666` (Muted Gray)

## Typography
*   **Headings:** A chunky, retro-style sans-serif or serif font (e.g., *Cooper Black*, *Bebas Neue*, or *Rokkitt*).
*   **Body Text:** A clean, readable sans-serif (e.g., *Inter*, *Roboto*, or *Space Grotesk* for a modern-retro twist).

## UI Elements
*   **Buttons:** Thick borders, solid colors, and harsh drop shadows (neo-brutalism style) or soft pill shapes with subtle inner shadows to mimic physical arcade buttons.
*   **Cards:** Off-white backgrounds with thick colored borders (Red, Yellow, or Green) depending on the card's context. 
*   **Borders:** Heavy and deliberate. 2px to 4px solid borders using the primary colors or deep charcoal.

## Design Philosophy
The design should feel tactile, bold, and slightly distressed. Avoid overly soft gradients; instead, favor solid blocks of color, bold lines, and high contrast.
